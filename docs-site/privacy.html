Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

ClipForge ("the app") is free, open-source software that you install and run on your own computer. It is developed by an individual developer and has no servers, no user accounts and no analytics. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored and how you can delete it.

1. YouTube API Services

ClipForge uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API) when you connect a YouTube channel. By using these features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

ClipForge does not access other people's channels, does not read comments or messages, and does not post anything you did not approve.

2. Instagram / Meta data

If you connect an Instagram professional account, ClipForge uses the Instagram API with Facebook Login to read your account's username, profile picture, follower count and the Facebook Page it is linked to, to publish Reels you approved, and to read insights for your own Reels (views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves). Meta's handling of data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy; Instagram use is subject to the Instagram Terms of Use.

3. Where your data is stored

  • Only on your own computer, inside the ClipForge folder: login tokens (workspace/channels/…/token.json, workspace/accounts/instagram/…/token.json), your clips, the publishing queue and statistics (workspace/clipforge.db), and settings (config.yaml, .env).
  • Nothing is sent to the developer of ClipForge or to any third party. The app only contacts Google (YouTube) and Meta (Instagram) for the accounts you connected, plus optional services you enable yourself (a local AI model, or a Telegram bot you created, which receives only the notifications you switch on).
  • Statistics and data derived from YouTube API Services are kept for at most the period allowed by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies and are refreshed or deleted within 30 days when they are no longer authorised.

4. How to revoke access and delete your data

  • In the app: Accounts → Disconnect. ClipForge revokes its access token at Google (or Meta) and deletes the saved login from your computer.
  • At Google: remove "ClipForge" at Google security settings → Third-party access (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
  • At Meta: remove the app in Facebook → Settings → Business integrations.
  • Delete everything: delete the ClipForge workspace folder (or the whole ClipForge folder). Because no copy exists anywhere else, this deletes all data ClipForge ever stored. Disconnect also deletes the statistics stored for that channel/account immediately. If you revoke access only at Google or Meta, ClipForge can no longer read anything and marks the account "needs re-login"; use Disconnect (or delete the folder) to remove what was stored.
  • Data request or question: open an issue at github.com/hamsidhi/clipforge/issues.

5. Children

ClipForge is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from them.

6. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the source repository with a new "last updated" date.
