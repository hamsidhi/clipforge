Long podcast in, ready-to-post Shorts out, on your own computer.

ClipForge is a free, open-source desktop tool (Windows, macOS, Linux). It finds the best moments in a long Hindi/English podcast, cuts them into vertical 1080×1920 clips with animated captions, and helps you publish them to your own YouTube channel and Instagram account on a schedule.

Personal use Runs locally No accounts, no servers, no ads

Uses YouTube API Services

ClipForge uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule Shorts on channels that you own and have connected yourself, to check their status and to show their statistics. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Read how ClipForge handles data in our Privacy Policy.

How it works

1. Make clips

Paste a podcast link or pick a file. Transcription (Whisper) and clip picking (a local AI model) run on your machine.

2. Review

Approve, trim and fix captions in the app. Edit every title, description and caption before anything is published.

3. Schedule

Pick the visibility (scheduled, public, unlisted or private) and the time. ClipForge uploads to the channel you chose; YouTube publishes at that time.

Your data stays with you

Login tokens, videos and statistics are stored only on your own computer, in the ClipForge folder. ClipForge has no server and sends nothing to its developer or any third party. It talks only to Google (YouTube) and Meta (Instagram), and only for the accounts you connect. You can disconnect at any time inside the app, or revoke access in your Google account / Facebook business integrations.
